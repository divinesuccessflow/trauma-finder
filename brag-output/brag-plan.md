# MandIMMO: brag plan

**What it is:** A €29/month tool for French real estate agents. Their flyers, signs and social posts turn into seller requests, and every request comes with timestamped proof of consent (double opt-in) that is Bloctel and RGPD compliant.

**Who it's for:** Independent agents ("conseillers") and agencies who prospect for listings (mandats).

**What sets it apart:** It doesn't sell cold lead lists. The owner comes to you, ticks the consent box themselves, and you get proof you can export.

**Angle:** Cold calls versus calls people are waiting for. The video starts on the pain of cold calling, then shows the reverse, using the site's own line: *"Appelez des propriétaires qui attendent votre estimation."*

**Hook:** Black screen, a phone shows an "Appel en cours…" call. Then the site's own copy lands: *"Vous appelez des propriétaires agacés qui n'ont rien demandé."* A red ✕ stamps it.

**Highlights:**
1. The owner's journey on a phone: scan the flyer QR code, describe the property, then tick the consent box (not pre-ticked), with an animated tap.
2. The lead receipt from the site: "Consentement confirmé", timestamps filling in, "Appeler le propriétaire".
3. Proof: "Registre de consentement · Export CSV en un clic" plus the Bloctel/RGPD, exclusive leads and no-commitment checks.

**Punchline:** *"Votre prochain mandat a déjà scanné votre flyer."* Then 29 € / mois · Sans engagement · mandimmo.com

**Tone:** `default`, leaning polished. Clean and confident, with soft transitions.
**Format:** Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps (Reels/TikTok/Shorts audience).

**Visual identity:** Cream background `#F5F3EE`, ink `#111821`, accent green `#0E6E55`, signal green `#3DDC97`, line `#DAD5CB`, muted `#4A5059`. Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque 800 for headlines, IBM Plex Sans for body, IBM Plex Mono for labels. Photos come from the landing-page export.
